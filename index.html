About Me

Hi, I'm Gianluca Frassoni, a final-year Computer Engineering student.

While my academic background has provided me with a solid foundation across the tech stack, my true expertise and passion lie in frontend development and UI/UX design. I am highly driven by the challenge of programming dynamic, interactive data visualizations and crafting intuitive interfaces that elevate the overall user experience.

Beyond coding, my lifelong background in team sports and music has instilled in me a strong sense of discipline, collaboration, and creative problem-solving. Furthermore, my deep passion for the video game ecosystem—both developing and analyzing them—has heavily influenced my approach to interactive design, giving me a sharp eye for user engagement and responsive logic.

I am currently seeking a stimulating role that will challenge my technical skills, allow me to contribute to innovative projects, and help me continuously grow as a professional.
